The 24 original artworks are by Twitter and other [Twemoji v14.0.2 contributors](https://github.com/twitter/twemoji/tree/v14.0.2/assets/svg). They are licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The local SVG files are original graphics. The PNG files are 360 px rasterizations on transparent backgrounds. Format tests also composite selected PNGs over white and encode them as JPEG at quality 88. No hand cleanup was used.

The first ten artworks formed the comparison set. Six different icons were first evaluated with v26, then the flower and cup were used to diagnose defects. Eight further icons were set aside for the v27 evaluation. Names, sizes, thread settings and format variants are fixed in `../public_icons.py`. These icons come from one collection.
